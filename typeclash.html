Why typography is the most underrated brand decision.
Every brand has a voice. Typography is how that voice sounds to the eye — before a single product is seen, before a price is quoted, before a handshake is extended. The pair you choose tells your audience whether you're authoritative or approachable, timeless or current, careful or bold.
