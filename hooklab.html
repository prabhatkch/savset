How the score works
15 PTS
Length
Short enough to read or say in about 3 seconds. The ideal range changes by format.
25 PTS
Curiosity
Open loops like questions, "why", "mistake" or "nobody tells you" make people stay for the answer.
25 PTS
Specificity
Numbers, timeframes and names beat vague words like "amazing" or "stuff".
20 PTS
Promise
A clear payoff: save time, avoid a mistake, learn a skill, get a result.
15 PTS
Voice
Speaks to "you", skips greetings and filler, and stays clean and readable.

HookLab is a rule-based checker. It spots patterns that tend to hold attention, but it cannot predict how a post will perform. Use it to draft faster, then test two versions and compare results.
