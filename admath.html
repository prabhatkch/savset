The math

Three formulas, no tricks. Everything runs in your browser.

01

Profit per sale

Price minus cost. This is the most you can spend to win one customer, so it is also your max cost per sale.

02

Max cost per click

Profit per sale × conversion rate. If a click converts 2.5% of the time, each click can only be worth 2.5% of your profit.

03

Break-even ROAS

Price ÷ profit. Below this return on ad spend you lose money; above it you profit. Cost per lead works the same, using your close rate.
